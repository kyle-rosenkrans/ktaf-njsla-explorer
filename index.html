    None of the grades picked are tested in this subject. Science is tested in grades 5, 8 and 11, and NJGPA in grade 11.

    Overall adds every ELA and Math score together (ELA grades 3–9, Math grades 3–8 and Algebra I), so a student who took both counts once in each, and counts are scores, not students.

    The NJGPA passing score was lowered after 2021–22 (from 750 to 725), so 2022 rates are not comparable with later years.

    The state's 2025–26 files list every KIPP Paterson student's race as "declined to report", so its 2026 results by race are missing here.

        About these figures
        • Source: the New Jersey Department of Education's statewide assessment data files for spring 2022 through spring 2026 (105 workbooks: every NJSLA ELA, Math and Science test and both NJGPA tests, each year), downloaded November 13, 2026 from nj.gov/education/assessment/results/reports. Nothing here comes from KIPP's own systems.
        • % proficient is the share of valid scores at Level 4 or 5 on ELA and Math, Level 3 or 4 on Science, and Graduation Ready on NJGPA.
        • KIPP Newark is TEAM Academy Charter School, KIPP Camden is KIPP Cooper Norcross Academy (a Renaissance school LEA) and KIPP Paterson is Paterson Prep Charter School, which first tested in 2024 and joined KIPP for 2025–26. The state reports each as one LEA, so results cannot be split by campus here.
        • District schools are the traditional district's own results. Charters and Renaissance schools are separate LEAs and are not in them.
        • Other charters in the city adds up every other charter and Renaissance LEA assigned to that city. A charter is assigned to the district that sends it the most students, using the assignments in the NJ Charter Sector Explorer.
        • Grades: pick a range or any mix. The page adds up scores and proficient scores across the tests picked. All grades pools ELA grades 3–9, Math grades 3–8 plus Algebra I, and Science grades 5, 8 and 11. Geometry and Algebra II appear only as grade rows.
        • Suppression: the state hides any group with fewer than 10 students, and sometimes hides a count while publishing the rate. Hidden results are never filled in, so a pooled rate covers only the published grades and is marked ◐ when some are missing.
        • Black & Latino adds African American and Hispanic students where the state publishes both. Group names changed over the years (for example English Language Learners became Multilingual Learners) and are matched up here.
        • 2026 ELA, Math and NJGPA results come from the new adaptive NJSLA-A and NJGPA-A, with new score scales. Science did not change. The NJGPA passing score was also lowered after 2022.
        • Gaps in the 2026 files: the state lists every KIPP Paterson student's race as "declined to report" and none as economically disadvantaged, and lists almost no Camden City district students as economically disadvantaged (0.5%, after 85% in 2025). About ten other LEAs show similar one-year swings. Results for those groups in 2026 are blank or unreliable for those LEAs.
        • Economic disadvantage in the scatter is economically disadvantaged tested students divided by all tested students across every ELA and Math test, taking the middle value of the years 2022–2026 so that a single misreported year does not move a dot. It is not an enrollment figure. A place is left out where the state hides the counts, which includes KIPP Paterson.
